Help & Support

Need help with your order or shopping? We're here to help.

🛒 How do I place an order?

Browse our products, choose the product you want, select the available options such as size or color, and add it to your cart. Then proceed to checkout and complete your payment.

💳 How can I pay?

Payments are handled securely through Paystack during checkout.

💬 How can I contact Yellow Cicy?

You can contact us through WhatsApp or call our support line.

📦 What happens after I place an order?

Your order is processed and prepared for delivery to your selected pickup station. You will receive updates as your order progresses.

❌ Can I cancel my order?

Orders can be cancelled when they are still within the cancellation period and have not reached a stage where cancellation is no longer available.
