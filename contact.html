❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?

Open your account and select My Orders. You can see your order status, shipping progress and pickup information there.

How do I contact Yellow Cicy about an order?

You can contact us through WhatsApp or call our support number. Please have your order number available when contacting support.

What payment method do you accept?

Yellow Cicy supports secure online payments through Paystack. Payments are processed in Ghana Cedis (GHS).

Can I cancel my order?

Eligible orders can be cancelled from My Orders. Orders that have progressed beyond the allowed cancellation stage may no longer be cancellable.

How does pickup station delivery work?

When your order reaches the selected pickup station, your order status will change to At Pickup Station. You'll receive the pickup location and deadline in your My Orders page.

Where does Yellow Cicy deliver?

Yellow Cicy is designed to serve customers across Ghana.
